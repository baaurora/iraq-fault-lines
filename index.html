    Built as a companion to Blowback season 1. Links point to declassified US records (Foreign Relations of the United States, National Security Archive, presidential libraries), UN Security Council documents, US laws and congressional reports, official investigations, and Human Rights Watch field research. Each blurb cites the document that best supports it. The "Why they did it" notes summarize each side's aims as stated in that document or widely documented in the historical record. They are interpretation, so contested points, such as the CIA's role in 1963, are marked as debated.
